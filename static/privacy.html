Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Your images

Both the JPG to WebP Chrome extension and the online converter on jpg-to-webp.pro convert images locally on your device. Your images are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see them.

The Chrome extension

The extension does not collect, store or transmit personal data, browsing history or the content of the pages you visit. Permissions are used only to show the right-click menu, read the image you chose to convert and save the converted file to your computer.

The website

jpg-to-webp.pro uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. We only see aggregated statistics such as page views and the number of conversions.

Feedback forms

If you choose to send feedback (for example after uninstalling the extension), the form is hosted by Google Forms and only the answers you enter are submitted.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support tab of the extension’s page in the Chrome Web Store.
